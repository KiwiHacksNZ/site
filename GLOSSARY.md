# KiwiHacks website

The public website of KiwiHacks, New Zealand's high school hackathon club. It tells the public what KiwiHacks runs and showcases the work and people that come out of it.

## Language

**Story**:
A written account of one or more young people's journey in tech, published on the website. A story can be about one person or a team.
_Avoid_: Profile, article, post, case study

**Showcase project**:
Something built at a KiwiHacks event, shown with a screenshot, its team and its placing. A story is a separate thing and is not a showcase project.
_Avoid_: Entry, submission

**Sign-off**:
The recorded agreement of a student, and their guardian where needed, to be written about in a story. A story is not published until its sign-off is recorded.
_Avoid_: Consent form, permission

**Tag**:
A topic label on a story, chosen from a fixed list. A story carries one to three tags.
_Avoid_: Category, keyword, label

**Region**:
The New Zealand region a story is placed in: the region of the person's school, or for a multi-person story the region where they competed, chosen at the site maintainer's discretion.
_Avoid_: Location, city
